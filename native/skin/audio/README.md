# Provisional procedural audio

14 original synthesized OGG/Vorbis files, 44.1 kHz. These are layered procedural sound-design assets, not outputs of ElevenLabs, Suno or Udio and not recordings of authentic acoustic instruments. Both 96-second music files wrap event tails and reverb for looping. Technical decode/peak checks passed; listening and in-game mix approval remain pending. No third-party audio samples are used.
